# Structure review

The visual rules in DESIGN.md say how things look. This review decides **what's there, in what order, and what it's called.** Run it before showing Himanshu a plan; its output goes in the plan.

## Steps

1. **Who and what job.** Name the person (Claire, Mike, Michelle) and list their jobs on this surface, most urgent and most frequent first. One line each.
2. **Keep / change / cut, for every existing item.** Never inherit the current structure. For each item (nav entry, column, section, email block, button), write one row:

   | Item | Job it serves | Keep / change / cut | Why |
   |---|---|---|---|

   An item that serves no job is cut. Two items serving one job become one.
3. **Order** the survivors: what needs you now → what Ally made for you → how Ally behaves → history last.
4. **Name** each item in the person's words. Check every label against the words already used in the product (DESIGN.md, the demo script): the same thing gets the same word everywhere. Flag system words.
5. **Check the nine rules** in DESIGN.md "Structure and naming". List any rule the proposal breaks, and why that's right, or fix it.
6. **Show it** in the plan: jobs list, the keep / change / cut table, the proposed structure with labels. Wait for approval before building.

## Signs the review was skipped

- A label the person has to translate ("Conversations", "What's waiting", "Asked").
- Two columns or sections answering one question (status and due date split across two).
- A heading that only names a group ("Work", "Make").
- An entry that leads nowhere, or a second way into the same thing.
