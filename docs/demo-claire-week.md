# Demo: Claire's week

**The point:** Claire, VP Ecommerce, can run her team's week entirely from email. Ally assigns, chases and ships. She sets the defaults. The product is there when she wants it, never required.

**Setup**
- Sign in to the demo with the inbox you'll show on screen. Real emails land there, and Mike's Slack DM goes to the Slack workspace wired for the demo.
- Open https://content-agent-demo-dusky.vercel.app/claire-waterfall and refresh. A refresh resets the week to Mon, Oct 5.
- Keep two tabs: your inbox, and Ally (Claire's page). Mike's page is one click away in the left bar.
- Presenter controls sit at the bottom right of Claire's page, in the Demo row.

## Beat 1 · Monday 8 AM: Claire hands out the week

| Who | Do | What the room sees |
|---|---|---|
| Claire | Click the **email icon** (top right) | Real email: "Mon: this week's work for your team · $1.5M one approval away". One line per person with their value: Mike $660K (expires in 21 days), James $560K, Michelle $312K. "Reply YES to assign all three, due Wed" |
| Claire | Show the email, then click **Claire replies YES** *(or **Assign** on each row of her page)* | Status: "Not started". Action: "✓ Assigned · due Wed" |
| Mike | Open Slack | Real DM: "Claire assigned you this week's work: Halloween seasonal updates and gift sets. Due Wed, Oct 7." |
| Mike | Open Mike's page | Halloween items read "Due Wed". The detail pane reads "Claire assigned this Mon · due Wed, Oct 7" |

**Say:** "Claire never logged in. She replied YES from her phone, and three people have their week."

## Beat 2 · Wednesday 8 AM: who's done, who isn't

| Who | Do | What the room sees |
|---|---|---|
| Presenter | **Go to Wednesday ▸** | James: approved. Michelle: "6 of 28 SKUs approved" ($204K left). Mike: "Not started". Halloween expires in 19 days |
| Claire | Show the real email | "Wed: 1 of 3 done · 2 for you to decide". Reply options: 1 · Mike: finish by Fri, or Ally ships it. 2 · Michelle: finish by Fri |
| Claire | **Claire replies YES** *(or **Assign · due Fri** on her page)* | Mike and Michelle: "✓ Assigned · due Fri". Under Mike's: "Ally ships it if not approved" |

| Mike *(optional)* | In Mike's queue, open **Halloween gift sets** and click **Approve 180 SKUs** | Claire's row: "180 of 414 SKUs approved" |

**Say:** "She didn't chase anyone. She set a default: finish by Friday, or Ally ships it."

## Beat 3 · Friday: Ally keeps Claire's word

| Who | Do | What the room sees |
|---|---|---|
| Presenter | **Go to Friday ▸** | Michelle: approved. Ally ships Mike's unapproved core SKUs and holds the 20 hero SKUs for him. One approval away drops to $355K |
| Claire | Show the real email | "Fri: 2 of 3 done · Ally shipped $305K on your deadline" ($205K if Mike approved the gift sets). One reply option: put seasonal content on autopilot |

**Say:** "Next time, nobody has to be chased. Her top sellers still get Mike's eyes."

## What each person does

**Claire**
- Monday: one reply to assign the week.
- Wednesday: one reply to set Friday's deadline.
- Friday: one click, or one reply, to make it the rule.
- She can do all of it from email or all of it on her page. Same moves, same result.

**Mike** (the presenter plays him in the cut-aways)
- Monday: gets the assignment in Slack, opens his queue and sees "Due Wed".
- Wednesday: gets Claire's Friday deadline in Slack.
- Optional, between Wednesday and Friday: approves the gift sets (180 SKUs) to show "Mike did half".
- Friday: sees what Ally shipped and the 20 hero SKUs waiting for him.

**James** (media; his page is `/james`)
- Nothing required on stage. His three plays are approved for him by Wednesday: fund the 14 keywords content added, the Winter Hearth launch campaign, and moving $24K from large jars to gift sets ($560K).
- Optional cut-away: his page shows the content-and-media loop. Pacing by category, keyword harvesting, and the keyword table both agents share. Mike's Halloween drafts show what media funds once they're live.
- Optional: ask "I have $500K more budget. How do I spend it?" on his page (or Claire asks "Where should I invest an additional $500K?" and sends it to him). Approving the plan clears his Needs your input row ($1.04M).
- No real Slack for James in the demo.

**Michelle**
- Nothing on stage. Her work is played for her: 6 of 28 SKUs approved by Wednesday, the rest by Friday.
- Don't cut to her page in this story. Her inbox shows each fix at its full value, while Claire's row shows $312K for this quarter, so the numbers won't match on screen.

## Before you present
- One full run sends 4 real emails. The limit is 20 an hour.
- If something goes wrong, refresh to start the week over.
- The emails' reply buttons are acted out with the Demo row. Ally doesn't read real replies.
